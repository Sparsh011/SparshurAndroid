WHY THE RANDOM-LOOKING TEXT?

Binary, wearing a text jacket.

The nonce and ciphertext use Base64 so JSON can carry their bytes. Base64 alone hides nothing. AES-GCM encrypts the original lab; ChaCha20-Poly1305 encrypts paired messages.

WHAT DOES THE RECEIPT PROVE?

Two different receipts.

Original lab receipts only acknowledge arrival. Paired-message receipts are signed by this Mac and checked on Android after successful authenticated decryption.

WHAT COMES NEXT?

A foundation to review.

This lesson adds one-way paired encryption. A production relay, multiple devices, reordering and forward secrecy remain future work. Human protocol review is required.
